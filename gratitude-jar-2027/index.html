Three small things, every day.

  1. 1

    Write it

    One line, or a whole page, on paper you choose: classic, heart, star or ticket stub. Small is perfect: a warm cup of chai, a call from home.

  2. 2

    Stick it

    Place stickers anywhere, tilt them, add a photo, and pick the mood of the day. Every month has its own paper color.

  3. 3

    Drop it

    Drag the slip into the jar. The lid closes with a clink and stays closed until 31 December.

Missed a day? The jar doesn't mind.

There are no streaks and no red warnings. The jar only counts what's inside it, so you see days in your jar, never days you failed.

Turn on a quiet evening reminder if you want one, and ignore it whenever you like. You can also go back and fill in the last few days.

    On 31 December, the jar opens.

    Every slip comes out with its sticker, one day at a time, and you read your year back to yourself, with a letter from past you at the end. Then you get a small recap: how many days are in your jar, your most grateful month, the words that kept showing up.

    Share the recap card if you want to. The notes themselves never have to leave the jar.
